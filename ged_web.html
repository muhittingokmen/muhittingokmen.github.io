GED Generalized Edge Detector

Canny edge detection with the Gaussian replaced by the membrane–thin plate kernel R(x; λ, τ) and its derivative by G = R′.

Image

Drop an image on the input plate or choose a file. Images larger than 1024 px are scaled down.

Kernel

Sets λ and τ to the kernel closest to a Gaussian of that σ: the least-squares fit, or the best non-negative (critical) fit.

Thresholds (grey levels / pixel)

Filters R and G = R′

—κ = B²/A
—A = λτ
—B = λ(1−τ)

dx = G(x)·R(y) ∗ I and dy = R(x)·G(y) ∗ I with 1-D kernels sampled on integers, R scaled to unit sum and G so the slope of a ramp comes out exact. Non-maximum suppression and hysteresis follow OpenCV's cv2.Canny with the L2 gradient; on the sample image this page reproduces cv2.Canny(dx, dy, …) pixel for pixel. A 3×3 Sobel responds 8× stronger, so an OpenCV threshold T equals T/8 here. Sample: scikit-image camera test image.
